About me

Engineer by trade, creator by habit.

I'm Abhishek Thakur, a Python Developer at Cognitensor and a Python full-stack developer with 2 years of experience building backend systems with Python, Django, Django REST Framework and PostgreSQL. Right now I'm working on Sustain3P, an ESG and sustainability management platform, where I build tenant-based features, ESG calculation workflows, automated reports and AWS S3 integrations. Along the way I've designed databases, optimised queries, integrated third-party services like Twilio and Razorpay, and shipped with Docker and CI/CD. I care about clean, maintainable code and turning business requirements into reliable, scalable software. Outside of code, I enjoy graphic and logo design and video editing.
